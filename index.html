The Story

Undergrad

Izabella was an attending student from 2021-2024 for a Bachelor's in Computer Science. while attending, she learned the fundamentals of programming and web development. She also attended a few hackathons and coding competitions, where she was able to apply her skills in a practical setting and win awards. Among peers, she was known as tutor for the computer science courses, where she would help students with understanding the material. Alongside being a student and tutor, she helped start the Data Structures and Algorithms Architects club, where she was the treasurer and helped students prepare for real world coding interviews.

Graduate

After completing undergrad, Izabella continued her education and pursued a Master's degree in Computer Science. From 2024-2026, Izabella dedicated her time not just as a Master's student, but also as a researcher. In her research, she explore path finding algorithms and spent time with flocking path finding algorithms as well. This was a challenging feat alongside the Master's degree, but with her research she was able to achieve the ERN 1st Place Graduate Oral Presentation Award in Atlanta, Georgia during the 2025 conference.
